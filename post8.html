A Shop for Killers Season 2 has unveiled a highlight teaser. 

A Shop for Killers follows Ji An ( Kim Hye Joon ), who, after enduring a grueling succession, has become the new head of the shopping mall. Reunited with her uncle Jin Man ( Lee Dong Wook ), she launches a counterattack against Babylon.

The highlight teaser captures Jin Man (Lee Dong Wook) and Ji An (Kim Hye Jun) launching their counterattack to protect everything they hold dear.

After learning the truth about Jin Man and the shopping mall for killers, Ji An wants to turn her back on it all and live an ordinary life. However, Babylon devises a plan to use her as bait to capture Jin Man.

Realizing all too clearly that there is no longer any way to avoid it, Ji An accepts her fate and takes her first step as the new head of Murthehelp.

Ji An's vow, "I will never do things the way Jeong Jin Man did. Never," offers a glimpse of her unwavering resolve not to lose the people she holds dear.

The intense action sequences that follow not only herald the all-out war between Murthehelp and Babylon, but also showcase the series' upgraded scale. As Babylon launches relentless attacks, Ji An's desperate cries and the moment when Jin Man erupts with emotion, shouting, "Protect Jeong Ji An, now!", hint at the unpredictable twists and turns that await in Season 2.

A Shop for Killers Season 2 will premiere with Episodes 1 and 2 releasing on July 22, with two new episodes released every Wednesday. Stream it on Disney+ and Hulu (select regions). Watch the teaser here .
