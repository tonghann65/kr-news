Liu Yu Ning's studio warns of legal action following Gong Jun controversy

 

On July 18, during the mud pit challenge on the variety show Wow the World, Liu Yu Ning, seemingly playfully, pushed the unsuspecting Gong Jun into the mud pit while they were not actively playing the game, drawing criticism from Gong Jun's fans.

Afterward, fans of both sides dug up past controversial clips involving the two, causing the situation to escalate.

That evening, Liu Yu Ning was the first to issue a public apology on Weibo, saying that he'll pay extra attention in the future to the things where I didn't do well enough.

He later addressed fans in a fan group, saying that he and Gong Jun had "already talked things over privately" and that "this isn't really a big deal between the two of us." He stressed that "we're also very good friends" and urged fans to "stop attacking others. Let's put this matter to rest here."

Gong Jun also promptly responded to the controversy with a voice message, emphasizing that "our relationship in private is very good." He expressed hope that fans wouldn't let these disputes affect their mood and wished for everyone to enjoy watching the show.

On July 19, the topics related to the incident climbed to the top of the trending searches.

On July 22, Liu Yu Ning's studio, through its retained law firm, issued a formal statement announcing that it had begun collecting and preserving evidence against the ongoing, coordinated dissemination of false and defamatory content targeting Liu Yu Ning across multiple online platforms. The statement made clear that the evidence obtained will serve as an important basis for the client's pursuit of legal action against those responsible, including filing complaints and reports, initiating civil lawsuits, and lodging criminal complaints.

According to the statement, certain netizens and online groups had been continuously, systematically, and in a coordinated manner publishing and spreading false and harmful information, misleading content, and defamatory remarks about Liu Yuning through posts, images, videos, the creation of topic hashtags, comments, and online interactions, among other means.

It further stated that some of the content was spread by quoting material out of context, selectively clipping footage from variety shows or livestreams, interpreting content without its full context, and selectively disseminating only certain portions, thereby stoking hostility, triggering unnecessary controversy and negative publicity, far exceeding the reasonable boundaries of legitimate public oversight and public discussion, and seriously disrupting the normal order of a clean and healthy online environment.

The statement demanded that netizens and online groups immediately stop publishing and spreading false, misleading, and defamatory content about Liu Yu Ning that infringes upon his legitimate rights and interests, delete any existing posts, and mitigate their impact. It also urged online platforms to fulfill their content moderation responsibilities by reviewing and removing infringing content, restricting its spread, and taking action against the accounts involved. The law firm warned that it would pursue civil and, where necessary, criminal legal action against individuals or entities that continue to spread defamatory content or infringe upon Liu Yu Ning's lawful rights and reputation.
