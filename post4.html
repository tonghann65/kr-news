Disney+ K-drama 'Made in Korea Season 2' announces premiere

 

Disney+ K-drama 'Made in Korea Season 2' announces premiere

by Lily Alice, July 22, 2026 · Updated July 22, 2026
14  4

The Disney+ original series Made in Korea Season 2 has confirmed its premiere for September and unveiled a poster and a teaser.

Made in Korea Season 2 tells the story set nine years after Season 1 and follows Baek Gi Tae (Hyun Bin), who has risen to the pinnacle of power; Jang Geon Young (Jung Woo Sung), who has been preparing his counterattack; and Baek Gi Hyun (Woo Do Hwan), who is carving out a different path to power from his brother.  

Following its achievement as Disney+'s most-watched Korean original of 2025, the series has continued to generate global anticipation, having also been selected by TIME magazine as one of the "Most Anticipated Korean Dramas of 2026."

The newly unveiled poster spotlights Baek Ki Tae in his new position as a director. Although he seized everything at the end of Season 1, the emptiness lingering in his gaze, combined with a nameplate bearing the title "Director Baek Gi Tae" and the striking tagline, "We are doing this for patriotism, for patriotism," foreshadows his relentless charge forward, carried out under the banner of patriotism.

The teaser opens with a shot of Baek Gi Tae seated in the presidential office, declaring, "I have waited for this moment for a very long time." As the action intensifies, the trailer introduces military forces confronting the Korean Central Intelligence Agency (KCIA), followed by the fierce standoff between Baek Gi Tae and Baek Gi Hyun (Woo Do Hwan), ratcheting up the tension.

In particular, the closing moments of the teaser capture Baek Gi Tae frozen in shock as Jang Geon Young (Jung Woo Sung) — who was defeated by Baek Gi Tae in the previous season — makes his unexpected return. The moment foreshadows a power struggle and a bloody showdown set to erupt between the two men.

Made in Korea Season 2 will premiere in September, only on Disney+. Watch the teaser here.
