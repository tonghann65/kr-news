Jung Ho Yeon to possibly star alongside Ryu Jun Yeol and Hong Kyung in new Netflix K-drama

by Lily Alice , July 22, 2026 · Updated July 23, 2026
9  10

Actress Jung Ho Yeon will possibly star in Netflix's upcoming K-drama Outback .

On July 21, JTBC Entertainment News reported, "Jung Ho Yeon will star in Outback . Through this project, she is expected to work alongside Ryu Jun Yeol and Hong Kyung ."

Jung Ho Yeon's agency, BH Entertainment, confirmed, "It is true that she has received the offer. She is currently reviewing it."

Outback follows Si On, who travels to Australia in search of his missing older sister, and Hans, a mysterious man he encounters there. 

The K-drama is based on the mystery thriller webtoon G'Day . Previous reports state that Ryu Jun Yeol and Hong Kyung are in talks to star in the series.

Outback is being produced by Shortcake, the production company behind the Weak Hero series. It will be directed by Jeon Do Gwan, who helmed TVING's Seen .
