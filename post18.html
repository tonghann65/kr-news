'Teach You a Lesson' actor P.O injured after being struck by car

by Lily Alice, July 22, 2026 · Updated July 23, 2026
24  11

Teach You a Lesson actor P.O (real name Pyo Ji Hoon), a member of the group Block B, was reportedly involved in a traffic accident in Jeju in which he was struck by a passenger car.

According to a Newsis report, P.O. was hit by a vehicle on a street in Jeju at approximately 9:56 p.m. on July 19.

After emergency responders were dispatched to the scene following a report of the incident, P.O. was taken to the emergency room of a general hospital in Jeju City. He is understood to have sustained injuries to his arm and knee. His injuries were not life-threatening, nor were they considered serious.

He was reportedly crossing the street when he was struck by the vehicle. Police are currently investigating the exact circumstances of the accident. Authorities have found no evidence of drunk driving or traffic signal violations.

P.O. is reported to have been in Jeju on vacation with acquaintances, although the details of his itinerary have not been confirmed.

P.O's agency Artist Company commented, "P.O recently went to Jeju Island on vacation with acquaintances and was involved in a traffic accident there. Fortunately, he was not seriously injured." They added, "His injuries are minor, and he is scheduled to participate in filming planned for next week as scheduled."

Source: Star News, Newsis
